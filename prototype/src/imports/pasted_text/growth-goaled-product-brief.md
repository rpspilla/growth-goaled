# Growth Goaled — AI-Powered GTM Intelligence Platform

Build a high-fidelity, interactive SaaS product prototype called **Growth Goaled**.

This is a prototype for a product serving **Series A B2B SaaS and AI companies with approximately $2M–$10M ARR**. The product helps founders understand how their go-to-market engine compares with high-performing peers, identify their biggest constraints, and determine what they should do next.

The prototype should feel like a **real, premium B2B SaaS product**, not a wireframe or generic AI dashboard.

The product has two user experiences:

1. **Founder Portal**
2. **Advisor / Admin Console**

The core product experience is:

**Company data → GTM profile → Benchmark → Baseline → ICP → Channel Strategy → Hiring Plan → Advisor Review → Action**

---

## 1. PRODUCT POSITIONING

Growth Goaled is not positioned as a generic AI consultant.

It is a **Growth Intelligence platform**.

The core promise:

> **Know where you stand. Know what to fix. Know what comes next.**

The product combines:

* Company-specific data
* Benchmark data
* Cohort comparisons
* AI analysis
* Methodology
* Source trails
* Human advisor review

The visual experience should communicate **intelligence, confidence, rigor, and simplicity**.

Avoid the visual clichés of AI products:

* No glowing robot imagery
* No excessive gradients
* No futuristic sci-fi UI
* No generic chatbot-first experience

Instead, make it feel like a sophisticated combination of:

* McKinsey-quality strategy work
* Modern SaaS analytics
* Premium financial dashboard
* AI-native workflow

# 26. DO NOT BUILD YET

This is a prototype, not the production application.

Do not spend effort on:

* Real payment processing
* Real CRM integrations
* Real Google Drive OAuth
* Mobile
* Social login
* Production backend
* Complex machine learning
* Real benchmark data infrastructure
* Public marketing website

Prioritize the **experience, storytelling, navigation, data visualization, and perceived intelligence**.

The goal is to create something that can be shown to founders and advisors to validate:

1. Is this valuable?
2. Would founders trust it?
3. Would founders give it their data?
4. Would advisors use it?
5. Would someone pay $25K+ for it?

Build the prototype around those questions.
